Who we are

SANRAKS Building Contracting works from Festival City in Ras Al Khor and takes on demolition, painting and building jobs across Dubai.

Many property owners need two contractors for one job: one to clear the space and another to make it look new. We do both, so there is no gap between the last skip leaving and the first coat going on.

Our crews work for landlords, property managers, facility companies and homeowners.
